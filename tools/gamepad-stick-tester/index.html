Rest drift is the largest stick reading while untouched; under 0.02 is good, above 0.08 usually needs a larger deadzone. Circularity error is the average gap between the stick's edge and a perfect circle, which matters for aiming and movement at full tilt. Magnetic sticks (Hall effect, TMR) should hold low drift over time; potentiometer sticks drift as they wear.

Inspection, with a narrow scope

All processing is local. Shows SHA-256, size and first bytes. UF2: checks every block’s magic values, payload bounds and numbering. Intel HEX: checks record lengths, checksums, EOF and nonoverlapping addresses. Mixed UF2 flags and nonstandard HEX formats may be unsupported.

Never connects to a keyboard, extracts installed firmware, executes a file, flashes, or infers model/version/support. A structural pass is not a compatibility or authenticity verdict. See inspection boundaries.

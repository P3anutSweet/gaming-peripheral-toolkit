Stereo channel calibration demo

Check channel routing and explore a listening balance.

Wear stereo headphones and begin with low system volume. Disable spatial effects for a simple channel check. Each tone lasts 0.7 seconds.

Audio starts only after a click.Browser-generated 440 Hz tone at low gain. Balance applies only to this demo. No microphone, hearing test, HRTF measurement, distance estimate or system-wide correction. Center is equal-power panning; device routing and mono accessibility settings may alter output.

What balance changes

Equal-power panning moves this page’s mono tone between stereo channels. Center feeds both channels. Balance and level affect only these demo tones; they do not adjust other apps or your operating system.

This is not a hearing test or measured spatial correction. Mono output, swapped wiring, virtual surround and device routing can change what you hear. No microphone or device permission is requested.

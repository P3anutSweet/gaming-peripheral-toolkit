Mouse sensitivity correction

A manual distance experiment, not a sensor measurement.

Mark a starting aim point. Measure mouse travel for one full in-game turn with a ruler. Repeat three times without lifting. Keep DPI and acceleration settings fixed.

Awaiting trials.New sensitivity = current × mean measured distance / desired distance. Retest after applying manually. This page never changes device/game settings. Ruler error, acceleration and inconsistent turns affect results.

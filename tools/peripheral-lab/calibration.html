Mouse sensitivity correction

Turn three ruler measurements into an adjustment.

Run the experiment

  1. Keep DPI fixed. Disable acceleration and use the same game/input mode.
  2. Aim at a recognizable point. Mark the mouse position and measure travel for exactly one full turn, without lifting.
  3. Repeat three times. Enter distances in centimeters, apply the suggested sensitivity manually, then retest.

A repeatable estimate

The adjustment uses current sensitivity × mean measured distance ÷ desired distance. A trial range over 10% of the mean is flagged for a repeat; this is a practical heuristic, not a statistical confidence interval. It does not measure sensor DPI or change device settings.
